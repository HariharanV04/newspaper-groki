## Dominant Themes (Oct 2026)

1. **Agentic workflows**  
   Long-running tasks, cross-conversation memory, goal persistence, and recovery mechanisms (Active Focus, agent memory systems, long-horizon RL).

2. **Efficiency at the frontier**  
   Low-active-parameter MoEs for on-device/edge use; accelerated diffusion (few-step Turbo checkpoints); distillation for real-time robotics.

3. **Open vs proprietary frontier**  
   Strong open-weight releases (LFM2.5, Qwen-Image, HF ecosystem) running in parallel with closed frontier models (Gemini 4 Argon, Muse Wonder).

4. **Multimodal everything**  
   Shared embedding spaces, vision-language MoEs, native multimodal frontier models, image generation + editing in the same pipelines.

5. **Safety & cyber applications**  
   Red-teaming of self-replication risks, deception benchmarks in software engineering, and frontier models positioned for cyber defense.
