High-level themes. Updated when the landscape shifts; not a daily dump.

## Dominant Themes (mid-Oct 2026)

1. **Large open-weight frontier models**  
   Muse Spark Open (816B/42B), Reflection Beam (501B), Nemotron 3.5 Super VL (120B/12B) bringing near-frontier capabilities to open weights, alongside proprietary Gemini 4 / Muse Wonder.

2. **Sparse MoEs + long context for agents & coding**  
   Low active parameters, 1M contexts, token-efficient designs enabling practical agentic and coding workflows.

3. **Efficiency & on-device / edge**  
   Few-active-param MoEs (LFM2.5, MiniCPM-V, Bolt), fused training for longer sequences, accelerated image models.

4. **Decision models & explicit outputs**  
   Shift toward choices + calibrated probabilities (JEV, GEV, Lumma-Fev) for reliable automation.

5. **Multimodal expansion**  
   Cross-modal embeddings, VLMs, image/video generation, native multimodal inputs.

6. **Agent safety, evaluation & long-horizon reliability**  
   Contextual integrity, harnesses, delivery benchmarks, multi-agent demos.

7. **Accessible compute**  
   Free tiers, ZeroGPU, NVIDIA endpoints, startup credits, and upcoming free Blackwell slices remaining essential for developers.
